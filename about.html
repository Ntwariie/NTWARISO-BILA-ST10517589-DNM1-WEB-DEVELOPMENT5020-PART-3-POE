About Paws & Hope Animal Rescue

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue is a hypothetical non-profit organisation developed for this assessment. It aims to support abandoned, neglected and vulnerable animals and help community members learn about rescue and responsible animal care.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome animals in need while encouraging responsible pet ownership.

Our Vision

To build a caring community where animals are treated with kindness and have opportunities for safe, suitable homes.

Our Values

  • Compassion towards animals
  • Responsible pet ownership
  • Community involvement
  • Respect and kindness
